# Progression

Ascend has two layers of progression: **the run** and **the player**.

## Run Progression

Temporary power is gained during a climb and disappears when the run ends.

This includes:

- Weapons
- Abilities
- Relics
- Temporary buffs
- Run-specific upgrades

## Permanent Progression

Long-term progression should expand possibilities without making new players feel permanently behind.

Potential permanent systems include:

- Unlocking new items into the pool
- New characters or starting options
- Cosmetics
- New tower content
- Account-wide discoveries

Permanent progression should support replayability rather than replace it.
