# Player Experience

## First 5 Minutes

The player should quickly understand three things:

1. They are climbing a tower.
2. They have a build that can change.
3. Their next choice matters.

## First Run

The first run should teach the core loop naturally through play rather than a wall of tutorials.

## Long-Term Feeling

Players should gradually move from:

> "What is this?"

To:

> "I know what I want my build to become."

And eventually:

> "Wait, I can combine **that** with **this**?"

## Success

A successful run should leave the player thinking about the next run before they have even left the tower.
