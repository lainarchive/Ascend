# World

The world of Ascend is centered on one impossible place: **the Tower**.

The outside world is intentionally less important than what lies inside the climb. The tower can contain environments that feel connected by a larger mystery rather than by normal geography.

## Tone

- Mysterious
- Strange
- Atmospheric
- Adventurous
- Occasionally surreal

## Worldbuilding Rule

Lore should reward curiosity without getting in the way of the game.

Environmental details, NPCs, encounters, enemies, and floor themes can tell the story naturally while the player focuses on climbing.
