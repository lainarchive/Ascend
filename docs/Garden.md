# The Garden

The Garden is Ascend's first playable map.

It is not just a forest-themed biome. The Garden is its own mini-roguelike with unique enemies, items, environmental interactions, Growth, and discoveries.

> The Garden isn't around the tower. The Garden is the tower.

## Progression

The Garden changes as the player climbs:

```text
normal
↓
overgrown
↓
ancient
↓
living
↓
impossible
```

## Ten-Floor Run

1. **The Clearing** — introductory combat and exploration.
2. **The Fork** — physical route choice.
3. **Overgrowth** — combat with stronger environmental interaction.
4. **Garden Shrine** — event and discovery-focused section.
5. **Bloomkeeper** — major elite encounter.
6. **The Merchant** — shop and build adjustment.
7. **The Forgotten Path** — stranger combat and older Garden elements.
8. **Unknown** — secret or unusual event.
9. **Heart of the Garden** — pre-boss section affected by the run's state.
10. **The Gardener** — map boss.

## Garden Enemy Roster

- Mossling — basic melee
- Petalbug — ranged
- Thornback — tank
- Rootbound — control
- Vine Stalker — ambush
- Bloomkeeper — elite
- The Gardener — boss

## Garden Weapons

- Wayfarer's Blade
- Rootbreaker
- Thornbow
- Thornwhip

Thornwhip is the first Garden-exclusive weapon.

## Garden Abilities

- Vine Lash
- Rootstep
- Poison Bloom
- Briar Crash
- Hunter's Instinct
- Ancient Growth

## Garden Relics

The first relic pool includes effects focused on:

- Thorns
- Flowers
- Roots
- Poison
- Ability/weapon interaction
- Aggressive combat
- Garden Growth

## Garden Mutations

The first mutation directions are:

- Thornblood
- Bloombody
- Rootbound
- Wildheart
- Venomous
- Ancient Growth

Mutations are rare discoveries rather than normal class selections.

## Growth

Player actions can influence the Garden's Growth state during a run.

Growth can change rooms, encounters, environmental hazards, rewards, and discoveries.

The goal is for the player to eventually feel that the Garden is reacting to what they are doing.
