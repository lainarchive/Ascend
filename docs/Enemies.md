# Enemies

## The Garden

The first map has a small enemy roster with clearly different roles.

### Mossling

Basic melee enemy.

- Fast
- Low health
- Appears in groups
- Simple close-range attacks

Purpose: teach basic combat and create pressure through numbers.

### Petalbug

Ranged enemy.

- Keeps distance
- Fires petals
- Low durability
- Becomes dangerous when protected by melee enemies

Purpose: force players to prioritize targets and move around the arena.

### Thornback

Tank enemy.

- Slow
- High health
- Heavy attacks
- Thorn-based defensive behavior

Purpose: punish careless attacks and give the player a durable target to work around.

### Rootbound

Control enemy.

- Creates roots
- Restricts movement
- Controls areas of the arena
- Works especially well with other enemies

Purpose: change positioning and make encounters less predictable.

### Vine Stalker

Ambush enemy.

- Hides around the environment
- Attacks suddenly
- Repositions after attacking
- Uses the Garden itself as cover

Purpose: make exploration and combat spaces feel alive.

## Elite

### Bloomkeeper

The Bloomkeeper is the Garden's first major elite.

It changes the arena while the player fights it.

Possible behaviors:

- Growing flowers
- Buffing nearby enemies
- Creating hazards
- Healing other Garden creatures
- Changing the arena over time

The goal is for the player to deal with the Bloomkeeper before the entire room becomes dangerous.

## Boss

### The Gardener

The Gardener is the Floor 10 boss of The Garden.

The exact boss fight is intentionally not finalized yet.

The boss should feel connected to the Garden itself rather than simply being a large enemy. The arena, environment, and Garden Growth system should all potentially matter during the fight.
