# Items

## Rarity

- Common
- Uncommon
- Rare
- Epic
- Legendary
- Mythic

Rarity controls frequency, not raw usefulness.

A Common item can be extremely valuable to a specific build. A Mythic item can be less useful when it does not fit the player's current build.

## Weapons

Weapons define how the player performs basic combat.

### Wayfarer's Blade

Fast, reliable sword with a basic combo and heavy forward slash.

### Rootbreaker

Slow hammer focused on stagger and area damage. Its heavy attack creates a shockwave and it can interact with certain Garden obstacles.

### Thornbow

Chargeable ranged weapon focused on precision. Charged shots can pierce enemies.

### Thornwhip

Garden-exclusive weapon focused on pulling enemies and creating roots.

## Relics

Relics are passive build modifiers.

### Gardener's Gloves

Flowers created by abilities last longer.

### Thorn Crown

Taking damage causes nearby thorns to erupt.

### Empty Seed

The next reward has an increased chance of containing something unusual.

### Root of the Ancient

Standing still for a moment creates roots around the player.

### Hunter's Fang

Rapidly defeating an enemy after damaging it with an ability briefly increases attack speed.

### Broken Compass

The first attack after entering a new room deals bonus damage.

### Echo Stone

Every few ability uses, the previous ability is partially repeated.

### Glass Thorn

Higher damage while at high health, with increased incoming damage.

### Bloomheart

Healing creates a flower nearby.

### Venom Root

Enemies affected by the player's roots become poisoned.

### Overgrowth

Plant-based effects become stronger based on the number of plants created during a floor.

### Predator's Mark

Enemies damaged by abilities become marked and can be affected by weapon attacks.
