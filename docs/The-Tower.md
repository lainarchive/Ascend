# The Tower

The tower is the world, progression system, and mystery at the center of Ascend.

It should feel ancient, impossible, and slightly different every time the player enters it.

## Possible Themes

- 🌲 Overgrown forest
- 🏚 Forgotten ruins
- 🪨 Deep caverns
- 🔥 Volcanic halls
- ❄ Frozen floors
- 🌊 Flooded chambers
- 🌌 Cosmic / void spaces
- ⛩ Ancient temples
- ⚙ Mechanical floors

These are a starting palette, not a locked content list.

## Vertical Progression

The tower should communicate progression visually as players climb. Higher floors can introduce stranger environments, tougher enemies, more dangerous modifiers, and increasingly unusual rewards.

## Mystery

The tower should raise questions before it answers them.

Why does it exist? Who built it? What is at the top? Why does it change?

The game does not need to answer everything immediately.
