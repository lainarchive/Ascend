# Design Pillars

## 01 — Every Run Is Different

Procedural floors, branching paths, rewards, events, and builds should keep runs from feeling identical.

## 02 — Builds Matter

A reward should change how the player approaches the next fight, not simply make a number bigger.

## 03 — Easy to Start

The first few minutes should be understandable without a wiki. Depth comes from combinations and decisions.

## 04 — Meaningful Choices

Choosing a room, reward, event, or upgrade should feel like a real decision.

## 05 — Co-op First

Playing with friends should create interactions that are difficult or impossible to get alone.

## 06 — Small Scope, High Replayability

Ascend should prioritize a small amount of strong, reusable content over a huge amount of shallow content.

## 07 — Strong Identity

The tower, presentation, creatures, builds, and progression should make Ascend feel like its own game rather than a generic roguelike template.
