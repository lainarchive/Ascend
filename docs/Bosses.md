# Bosses

Bosses are major milestones in the climb.

A boss should test more than raw damage. It should ask the player to understand its patterns, use the strengths of their build, and work with their team.

## Boss Goals

- Clearly telegraphed attacks
- Distinct identity
- A memorable arena
- Mechanics that reward adaptation
- Strong rewards
- A sense of progression after victory

The exact floor cadence and boss roster are not locked yet.
