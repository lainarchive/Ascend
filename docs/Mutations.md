# Mutations

Mutations are rare discoveries that can fundamentally alter a run's build.

They are not permanent classes or races, and they are not ordinary loot tiers.

> You discover a mutation because your run created the conditions for it.

## Philosophy

Mutations should create noticeable changes to gameplay, build identity, and sometimes the player's appearance.

Some mutations can be hidden discoveries. The player may not know the exact conditions required to reveal them.

Mutations can also be map-exclusive, allowing each map to develop its own identity.

## Garden Mutations

### Thornblood

Requirement direction: Thorn effects + taking damage.

Effect direction: attacks generate additional thorn effects around enemies.

Fantasy: the player is slowly becoming part of the Garden.

### Bloombody

Requirement direction: create many flowers.

Effect direction: flowers begin growing across the player's character and flower-based effects can interact with the player.

### Rootbound

Requirement direction: heavy investment in Root effects.

Effect direction: the player's movement becomes more restricted while gaining stronger attacks, root effects, and resistance to knockback.

### Wildheart

Requirement direction: Predator-style combat.

Effect direction: rapid kills can trigger a temporary feral combat state with faster movement and more aggressive attacks.

### Venomous

Requirement direction: Poison + Root.

Effect direction: poison spreads between nearby rooted enemies.

### Ancient Growth

Requirement direction: extremely high Garden Growth.

Effect direction: the player develops bark, vines, leaves, and other plant growth while gaining stronger plant interactions.

## Discovery

Some mutations should appear as discoveries rather than normal reward choices.

Example:

```text
FLOOR 3

"something has changed."

MUTATION DISCOVERED
BLOOMBODY
```

Failed runs can still contribute to discovery progress and knowledge, encouraging experimentation on future runs.
