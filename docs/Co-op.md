# Co-op

Ascend is designed around **2–4 players**.

## Playing Together

Players should be able to contribute in different ways rather than needing identical builds.

Possible co-op interactions:

- Reviving teammates
- Combining abilities
- Covering different combat roles
- Shared discoveries
- Build interactions between players
- Decisions made as a group

## The Goal

Co-op should add stories to a run.

The ideal result is the kind of moment where a group looks at what just happened and goes: **"how did that even work?"**

Solo play can exist, but multiplayer should feel intentionally supported rather than bolted on later.
